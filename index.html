Who we are

Science, Technology, an Engine for Civic Optimization

We make attempts at simple and yet profound optimizations to the civic order, focusing on specialty software and machinery to save the taxpayer and the business owner their money.

In our view, in the world of high technology the ceiling on inventions which can grease the wheels of industry and civil life is infinite. It simply remains for the entrepreneurial spirit to put their minds and bodies to the task of constructing solutions and marketing them. We believe that capital recognizes the proper value of products, and that it is the ingenuity and training of the entrepreneur that ultimately determines the quality of the products — and thus the market valuation and revenue. In line with this philosophy we work to maximize customer value through genuine innovation, creating real, sustainable wealth and long-term trust.

For us, the major problems of the engineer and the software developer over the next decade amount to making our infrastructure intelligent: to construct perfect traffic systems, a perfect rail schedule, perfect supply chains — achieved by a melding of our technology with our urban infrastructure. We believe this will increase security, efficiency and quality of life, and that the value to both the state and private industry is self-evident. We keep our team extremely small and choose it carefully, focusing on product schematics and software protocols without delving into manufacturing. This minimizes cost and lets us do what we do best: innovate.

Rooted in Halifax's coastal engineering heritage and Canada's growing deep-tech ecosystem, we build with the intent to provide value to the final buyer — be it government, industry, or individuals. Our sustainability and economic impact are, in our view, tied to our capacity for innovation, and to spotting problems where others do not.

  • Applied. Research that ships, not research that sits on a shelf.
  • Integrated. Hardware and software designed as one system.
  • Economical. Every project pre-assessed against real-world value to buyers.
  • Canadian. Building world-class capability from the Atlantic coast.
